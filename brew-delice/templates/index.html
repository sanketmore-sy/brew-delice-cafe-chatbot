☕ About us

A local, customer-oriented café

{{ biz.business_name }} is a local café in Pradhikaran, Nigdi, Pimpri-Chinchwad, owned by {{ biz.owner }}. The focus is simple: good coffee, honest prices and quick, friendly service for students, families and professionals nearby.

This website and AI chatbot are a digital-marketing prototype that helps customers discover the menu, check prices, find the café and reach out — 24×7, without waiting for a reply.

📍 Find Us

How the chatbot supports the café

  1. Customer engagement — greets visitors and answers questions instantly.
  2. Product discovery — menu, Main Attractions and prices in a tap.
  3. Ordering intent — “Order Now” routes to the café's verified ordering channel.
  4. Enquiry capture — name, contact and message stored for follow-up.
  5. Analytics — every step is measured on the admin dashboard.
